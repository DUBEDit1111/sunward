    How the sieve works

    Every story comes from a real newsroom. We read their feeds, set aside stories about violence, disaster, politics or loss, and keep stories where something got better.

    Good-news outlets pass with a light check. General science and environment desks need a clearly hopeful headline.

    Many stories carry a tag for how solid the evidence is: Early research (lab or animal work, a first study), Trial or pilot, Proven at scale, or Now policy. Some include a line on how you can help, only when there is something real to do.
